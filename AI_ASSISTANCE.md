# AI assistance and human authorship

This repository documents a human-directed research conversation with OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-6). The human researcher posed the problem, directed the successive proof and verification tasks, and requested reproducibility and an English publication package. ChatGPT contributed to the mathematical arguments, constructive search strategy, code generation, execution and interpretation of computations, certificate design, documentation and manuscript preparation.

Suggested disclosure for the publication:

> This research was conducted by the author with substantial assistance from OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-6). The model assisted in deriving mathematical arguments, implementing and running the certificate-generation and verification software, constructing proof games, and drafting the manuscript. The results are supported by the supplied reproducible certificates and checkers. The human author is responsible for reviewing the work and its claims.

Do not claim completed human review until the evidence has actually been reviewed. The model is acknowledged as a tool, not represented as an independent researcher, peer reviewer or signatory. The source files, test outputs and proof certificates are the auditable evidence; prior conversational statements are not evidence on their own. No endorsement by OpenAI, FIDE or any cited researcher is claimed. The human author should check the applicable publication's authorship and AI-disclosure policy before submission.
